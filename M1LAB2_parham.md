# The Peanut Butter Sandwich

## Assumptions
- The bread, the peanut butter jar, and knife are all present.

## Steps
- open the peanut butter jar
- take off seal on peanut butter
- untwist the tie on the bread bag
- open the bag
- take out loaf of bread
- put loaf of bread on counter
- grab the knife
- cut two slices of the loaf with the knife
- put the knife on the counter
- put one slice of bread on a plate
- grab the knife
- scoop out peanut butter with the knife
- spread the peanut butter on the slice of bread on the plate
- put the knife down
- put the second slice of bread on top of your plate

## Done When
- bread / peanut butter / bread combination
